Biography

Childhood

Zuko SA, whose real name is Zuko Kupiso. Born 1995 and raised in Dutya in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. He comes from a deeply spiritual and musical family. His father is a pastor, and he grew up listening to his parent's gospel collection alongside his twin brother and other siblings, who are also musically gifted.

Early Career

He learned to play the piano at his local church at age nine and began performing at school and regional arts festivals.

Though his parents encouraged him to get a qualification, he enrolled to study sound engineering at Revolution Media College in Johannesburg but was forced to drop out due to financial constraints. He subsequently taught himself how to produce and mix audio using YouTube.

Archievements

2022, Zuko SA experienced a major breakthrough with Qhawe Lam, a track from his debut album No one is Safe

The song featured his collaborator Nwabisa G (Nwabisa Gcilitshana). Sadly, just as the song blew up nationally as the main love then on The Wife, Nwabisa tragically passed away in a drowning accident.

Nominations

His highly successful sophomore studio album, Umkhonto, released in January 2024, received a prestigious South African Music Award
